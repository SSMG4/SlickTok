    What this is

    SlickTok is a tool that, given a public TikTok link, retrieves the underlying video (or photo slideshow) file and returns it to you without the watermark TikTok normally overlays for shared copies. It does not host, index, or archive any TikTok content of its own, it fetches on request, on your behalf, and discards the result afterward.
