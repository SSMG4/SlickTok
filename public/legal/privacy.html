Privacy Policy

SlickTok is built to need as little data about you as possible.

No accounts

There is no sign-up, login, or user profile anywhere on this site. Nothing you enter is tied to an identity beyond your IP address, and only for the purpose described below.

What is processed, and why

  • The TikTok link you paste is sent to the server to be resolved and is not stored, beyond the duration of that one request.
  • Your IP address is used to enforce hourly/daily rate limits and is held in memory only until its time window expires. It is not written to disk.
  • Video/image bytes are streamed from TikTok's CDN through the server straight to your browser, and are never saved to disk on the server.

What is not collected

No analytics, no tracking pixels, no third-party scripts, no advertising, no cookies. The only thing stored in your browser is your language choice, saved locally via localStorage, and it never leaves your device.

Third parties involved

To do its job, SlickTok's server necessarily talks to TikTok's own infrastructure. Your browser never talks to TikTok directly. TikTok sees the server's IP address, not yours.

Logs

The application does not log the URLs you submit or the videos you download. An instance operator may still have process-level operational logs.

Children's privacy

This service is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the project changes.
