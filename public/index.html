    What SlickTok does

    No watermark

    Downloads use TikTok's own clean playback file, nothing stamped on top.

    Ad-free, always

    No ad slots, no popups, no "watch an ad for HD." It's just the tool.

    Open source

    Every line is public. Read it, fork it, self-host your own copy.

    No account

    Nothing to sign up for. Rate-limited by IP, not by login.

    HD when available

    If TikTok served a higher-resolution version, SlickTok offers it too.

    Photos & slideshows

    Photo posts are supported on a best-effort basis, background music included.

    Questions

    Does SlickTok add a watermark of its own?

    No. The file you get is the same one TikTok's own app plays back internally, before any watermark is added for sharing.

    Is SlickTok safe to use?

    Yes. Nothing is installed on your device, there's no login to compromise, and the code is fully open source, so anyone can check exactly what it does.

    Do I need an account?

    No. There's no login anywhere on this site. Usage is limited per IP address instead, to keep the server available for everyone.

    What links are supported?

    Full links like tiktok.com/@user/video/123, and short links like vm.tiktok.com/xxxxx/.

    Is this affiliated with TikTok?

    No. SlickTok is an independent, unaffiliated open-source project. It is not made, endorsed, or supported by TikTok or ByteDance Ltd.

    Why does a download sometimes fail?

    Usually because the video is private, deleted, region-locked, or TikTok changed something on their end. Try again in a bit, or check for a newer yt-dlp release if you're self-hosting.

    Does downloading a video affect the creator?

    SlickTok doesn't like, follow, comment, or notify anyone. The creator isn't told that their video was downloaded. Please still credit and support creators whose content you save.

    Can I run my own copy?

    Yes, that's the point. The full source and a deployment guide are in the repository linked in the footer.

    Am I allowed to download any video I want?

    Only download videos you own, have permission to save, or that you're viewing for personal, non-infringing use. See the Terms for details.
